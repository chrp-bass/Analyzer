## Photo treatment recipe (to make any market photo brand-consistent)
Calibrate to `girl2.png`:
1. Tight crop on the subject (head/shoulders or the signature gesture), 4:5.
2. Desaturate hard and collapse to a cool **tritone** (deep indigo shadows → blue/violet mids
   → cool-white highlights) so clashing source colors unify into one mood.
3. Darken; crush the background so the subject reads.
4. Screen-add the **aura burst** low-center (magenta, with warm + green hints) and a cool blue
   haze up top.
5. Vignette to sink the background; fine grain.
6. Fade edges into the dark surface so it melts into the page rather than sitting in a box.
