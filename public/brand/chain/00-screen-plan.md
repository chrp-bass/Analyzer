# CHRP Page Screen — what informs it

The screen exists to make every CHRP site 10X more effective — visually, in copy, and in
brand authority — and to do it consistently enough that pages reach near-final in one pass.

Five inputs shape it.

**1. The brand, as truth.** Brand Guidelines V1A set the non-negotiables: the logo and bird,
Tiempos Fine + Lato, the core colors with CHRP Yellow as the signal, the color-burst aura,
and photography of real people. These are the connective tissue every surface carries.

**2. The two main sites as benchmark.** chrp.ai and mychrp define the family look and the
shared components (marquee, dividers, lexicon). Product and niche surfaces tie back to these;
they don't copy them.

**3. The audience, first.** Each surface serves one market — creators, pro and college
athletes, Navy commanders, medical reps, wellness leaders. The page belongs to that
audience's identity and desire. Make it about them: CHRP is the vehicle to what they already
want. A market may bring its own font, hero image, color, or iconography, as long as it
complements the core.

**4. The posture: a billion-dollar company serving, not selling.** Confident authority so
obviously valuable it takes orders rather than convinces. Sophistication and restraint over
hype. The EPI Score carries the authority.

**5. The human test, as the gate.** For the real visitor in that market: are they excited
this exists, do they trust it enough to act immediately, and would they forward it to a peer
saying "this is a game changer"? If not, the page hasn't earned its grade.

From these come the floors a screen may never breach: connectors present (logo, EPI system,
Yellow signal, aura, lexicon); cutting-edge and native to the audience (edge); billion-dollar
sophistication; the audience visibly present (human); and readable (contrast). A
recommendation that lifts compliance while dropping below any floor is wrong — re-anchor,
don't strip.

Two operating rules keep it efficient. **Reuse before recreate:** if a graphic exists in
`/brand` or `/brand/graphics`, pull it for consistency rather than building a new one.
**Additive over subtractive:** prefer using Lato Black, saturating the brand palette,
activating the aura, and reserving Tiempos for sophistication, over deleting faces and muting
color, which is how pages flatten into brochures.

The screen ends in a mockup, not a memo. Recommendations are easy to misread; a rendered page
proves the direction and exposes problems — blank slots, weak hierarchy, lost humanity —
before they reach the build. The handoff thread mocks up too, so the origin owner confirms
alignment before any Claude Code runs.

In short: the screen is informed by the brand's truth, the family's benchmark, the audience's
desire, a billion-dollar serving posture, and a gut-level human test — enforced through
floors, reuse, additive fixes, and a mockup at every step.
